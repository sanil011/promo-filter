Privacy Policy

Promo Filter for WhatsApp Web · Last updated 25 November 2026

Promo Filter is a browser extension that sorts your WhatsApp Web chat list into Chats, Groups and Promotions tabs. This policy explains what information the extension handles, what it sends to our server, and how to delete it.

The short version

  • Your messages never leave your browser. The extension reads them on your computer to sort your chats, and never uploads them.
  • To use the extension you sign in with Google. We receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google.
  • When you choose “Mark as promotion” or “Not a promotion” on a chat, that chat’s phone number and your choice are saved on our server, linked to your account.
  • Numbers that 3 or more users marked as promotions are shared with every signed-in user as a community spam list, without saying who marked them.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking. Nothing is sold.
  • The extension never sends messages or changes anything in your WhatsApp account.

1. What the extension reads in your browser (never sent)

While web.whatsapp.com is open, the extension reads, inside your browser only:

  • your chat list: chat names, phone numbers, profile photos, unread counts, and pinned, archived and status information,
  • recent messages of each chat, to show a preview and to decide whether the chat looks promotional (for example offers, discount codes or KYC messages).

This is used only to draw the sorted chat list on your screen. It stays in the tab’s memory, is gone when you close or reload the tab, and is never sent to our server or anyone else. Message text, chat names and your contact list are never uploaded.

Profile photos are loaded from WhatsApp’s own servers, the same way WhatsApp Web loads them.

2. Your account (sent to our server)

Signing in is required to use the extension. When you sign in with Google, Google shares with us:

  • your name, email address and profile picture link,
  • a Google account identifier.

We use these only to create your account, show who is signed in, and link your choices to you. We do not access your Gmail, contacts, Drive or any other Google data.

3. Chats you mark (sent to our server)

When you pick “Mark as promotion” or “Not a promotion” in a chat’s menu, the extension sends to our server:

  • that chat’s phone number (for example +919876543210),
  • your choice (promotion or not a promotion),
  • and the server records when it was made.

Nothing else about the chat is sent: no name, no messages, no photo. Choosing Undo deletes your choice for that number from the server.

Group chats, and chats where WhatsApp hides the phone number, are never sent. Your choice for them is saved only in your browser.

Free version limit: a free account can mark up to 5 numbers as promotions. The server counts your marks to apply this limit.

4. The community spam list

Our server combines everyone’s choices. A number that 3 or more users marked as a promotion goes on a shared list, together with how many users marked it. Every signed-in user receives this list, so those chats move to Promotions for them automatically.

The list contains only phone numbers and vote counts. It never shows which users marked a number. Your own choices are visible only to you.

5. What is saved in your browser

The extension uses Chrome’s built-in extension storage, on this computer only, for:

  • your settings (filter on or off, smart detection on or off),
  • the chats you moved by hand (their WhatsApp ID, which can include the phone number, and their display name),
  • your sign-in session (access tokens, your name, email and profile picture),
  • a copy of your own choices and of the community spam list, so the chat list loads quickly.

Signing out deletes the sign-in session and both copies. Uninstalling the extension deletes everything in this storage.

7. What we never do

  • We never upload your messages, chat names or contact list.
  • We never sell or rent your data, and never use it for advertising.
  • We use no analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • The extension runs only on web.whatsapp.com and reads no other website.
  • The extension never sends WhatsApp messages and never archives, mutes, blocks or deletes anything on its own. Menu items like Archive or Delete belong to WhatsApp and run only when you pick them.

8. How long we keep data, and how to delete it

  • One choice: use Undo after marking, or mark the chat the other way. Undo deletes the choice from the server.
  • Everything in this browser: sign out, or uninstall the extension.
  • Your account and all your choices on our server: email us at the address below from the email address you signed in with. We delete them within 30 days.

We keep your account and choices until you delete them or ask us to.

9. Children

The extension is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will change. If the change affects what data is sent to our server, it will also be described in the extension’s release notes.
